About

A small shop that would rather fix a thing than sell you another one.

Ridgeway started in a two-car garage on Vine Street with a lathe, a parts drawer and a backlog of survey crews whose meters had stopped agreeing with each other. Eleven years later we are nine people in a building off Ridgeline Court, and we still answer the phone ourselves.

We only make the R1 and the R2. That limit is deliberate. One product means one set of spare parts, one calibration procedure and one firmware track, so a unit bought in 2016 and a unit bought this year share the same battery door, the same probe connector and the same manual. It also means we can hold stock for fifteen years without guessing.

Everything we sell is designed to be opened with a hex key. If a unit arrives needing a repair we cannot do, we say so before you pay for the estimate.

Services

Four things we do

Most customers come for the first one and stay for the second. The last two are how we fill the winter.

  1. 01

    Calibration and certification

    Every unit, new or used, goes on the bench against a NIST-traceable reference. You get a certificate with the as-found and as-left readings, which is the part most shops leave out and the part your auditor will ask for.

    Five day turnaround · NIST-traceable references · Paper or PDF

  2. 02

    Field repair

    Swapped seals, tired o-rings, corroded connectors, cracked screens, dead batteries. We keep parts for every instrument we ever sold, including units built by other companies where we can still find a schematic.

    Flat bench fee · Parts list with prices before we start · Turnaround quoted same day

  3. 03

    Contract manufacturing

    Tooling, short runs and full production for outside brands. Your drawings, our shop. First article inspected against every dimension on the print before anybody cuts aluminium at volume.

    Runs of 50 to 5,000 · Inspection report per lot · Two-week quote

  4. 04

    Training and documentation

    Half a day with your crew in the shop or on your site: reading the instrument properly, cleaning the probe without ruining it, and knowing which readings to distrust. We also write field manuals.

    Half day, on site · Crews of up to eight · No charge under warranty
